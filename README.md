Two lines to my past self: a warning about one specific moment — a 2 a.m. text sent as a joke and heard as goodbye — and why it mattered. The program writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Don't send that 2 a.m. text to Maya from the taxi outside the airport — you mean it as a joke, and she hears it as goodbye.
> She never answers, and you spend the next three years learning that funny and final are not the same thing.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
